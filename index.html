Good questions

Straight answers.

Does it actually look like my dog?

Usually, yes — markings, eye colour and ear shape are kept. If the first three previews miss, you don't pay, and you can ask for another three free.

Where does the money go?

£15 from every digital portrait, print, mug and blanket goes to the kennels. The rest covers the printing and the postage.

Can I use a photo of a dog who's no longer with us?

Please do. Memorial portraits are the ones we're asked for most, and we take extra care with them. If a photo is old or grainy, send it anyway and we'll tell you honestly what will work.

What happens to my photo?

It's used to make your portrait and then deleted after 30 days. We never use customer photos in our marketing without asking you first, in writing.

Is this painted by a person?

No — the artwork is generated with AI from your photo, then checked by a real person before it's sent to you. We say so plainly because it matters.
